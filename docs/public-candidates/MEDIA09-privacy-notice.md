# Press and institutional inquiries: privacy notice

Content Catalyst LLC operates Sustainable Catalyst. For questions about your information or to exercise privacy rights, contact **tariq@sustainablecatalyst.com**.

## Information and purposes

We use the name, reply email address, inquiry category, subject and message you provide to assess and respond to your request and manage relevant institutional correspondence. Authorized staff may record assignment, follow-up, correspondence dates and review status. Please provide only information needed for your request. Do not submit passwords, identity documents, sensitive personal information or private research.

Where GDPR applies, ordinary Press, research collaboration and institutional partnership correspondence relies on legitimate interests in responding to requests and coordinating institutional relationships. Where you personally request steps toward a contract to which you would be a party, the applicable contractual basis may apply instead. We consider the necessity of the processing and its effect on your rights.

Acknowledging this notice does not give marketing consent, subscribe you to a newsletter or authorize CRM disclosure. Your inquiry is private; it is not published. HubSpot synchronization is not enabled for this form.

## Retention

Spam, invalid and rejected inquiries are deleted within 30 days. Routine answered or closed Press inquiries are deleted 12 months after the last substantive correspondence. Active inquiries are reviewed after six months without substantive activity and deleted when no longer needed. Established institutional relationships are reviewed annually and deleted after 24 months without meaningful activity unless a documented continuing purpose or legal obligation justifies further retention.

Closed privacy-request correspondence follows the ordinary 12-month retention period. Security and access logs are retained for the minimum operational period, ordinarily 30–90 days. Deletion receipts contain counts, reason and time rather than message contents or contact identifiers and are retained for up to 24 months where necessary.

A documented legal hold may delay deletion and is subject to review. Deleted records in retained backups are excluded from ordinary use and suppressed if a backup is restored. Managed backups expire within 90 days, except where a documented legal obligation requires preservation. Restricted, keyed deletion markers are retained while needed to prevent restoration of erased information from surviving backups.

## Service providers and international processing

Hosting, backup and security providers process information to operate and protect the service. Processing may take place in the United States and other countries. Further information about the relevant providers, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards is available through the privacy contact above and our [Privacy Policy](https://sustainablecatalyst.com/privacy-policy/).

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or a structured copy of your information, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and complain to the relevant supervisory authority. Rights and erasure may be subject to legal exceptions. We verify identity proportionately before disclosing or changing information; do not send identity documents through this form.

Where GDPR applies, requests are normally answered within one calendar month. Any permitted extension and its reason will be explained. Contact **tariq@sustainablecatalyst.com** or use the privacy-request interface linked from the inquiry form.
